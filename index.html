Artificial Intelligence(AI)

What is AI?

AI is the ability of computers or machine to perfoam task that normally require human intelligence such as learning problem solving and dicision making.

Types of AI

Meachine Learning

Machine Learning is a part of AI where computers learn from data instead of being directly programmed. The system learns from examples and makes decisions on its own.For Example, Instagram learns your choice and shows you similar videos.

Deep Learning

Deep Learning is an advanced part of Machine Learning that works like the human brain. It has many layers that understand data deeply. This is the reason behind face recognition, voice assistants, and self-driving cars.

Generative AI

Generative AI is a type of AI that creates new content like text, images, and videos. It learns from old data and generates something completely new. Tools like ChatGPT and image generators are the best examples of Generative AI.
